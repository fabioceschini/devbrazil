Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does dbMoney work without an internet connection?

Yes, dbMoney is installed locally on your computer and does not depend on an internet connection to function. dbMoney uses the internet (when connected) solely to display news, usage statistics, and check for updates. We do not collect any personal data.

2. How do I purchase the Premium version?

The Premium version can be purchased directly on the dbMoney home screen by clicking the Premium button (located at the top). The purchase and payment management are handled directly through the Microsoft Store. We do not have access to any payment details provided.

3. Is the Premium payment a monthly subscription or a one-time fee?

One-time fee—no subscriptions, lifetime use!

4. If I format my PC or switch computers, do I need to buy the Premium version again?

No, your license is linked to your Microsoft account. Once you log in with your Microsoft account on your new computer, your Premium license will be restored.

5. What happens if I use the Basic (free) version? Is there a time limit?

No, you can use the free version for as long as you need. However, it includes feature limitations, which are listed in the Plans & Versions section.

6. How does file management work in dbMoney?

Data files generated by dbMoney use the .dbm format (e.g., JohnSmith.dbm) and follow the same concept as an Excel spreadsheet. The data file features end-to-end encryption and can only be opened by dbMoney (you can also set a password for added security). You can create multiple data files, one for each purpose (personal, business, etc.).

7. Can I store and use my files in the cloud?

Yes, just like an Excel file, you can store and use your data files in your own cloud storage (OneDrive or Google Drive) for backup purposes or to access them on other computers.

8. Does dbMoney have an automatic backup feature?

Yes, dbMoney allows you to manage automatic or manual backups, fully configurable within the app settings.

9. Can I automatically import bank statements?

Yes. dbMoney allows you to import bank statements in the standard OFX format, eliminating the need to manually enter every transaction. It also includes automated tools for pre-filling information and detecting duplicate entries.

10. How do I keep my data safe when switching computers?

You can create backups with a single click and save them to your preferred location or your own cloud storage (OneDrive or Google Drive) to easily restore them on another computer.

11. Can I use dbMoney in multiple languages or currencies?

Yes, dbMoney features native support for multiple languages and currencies, making it ideal for both local and international use.

12. How does technical support work if I need help?

Technical support can be reached at any time via email at support@devbrazil.com or through the contact form on this page.

dbNotes    Windows  100% Free  In Development

dbNotes is the ultimate application for academia, technical manuals, documentation, and structured writing. Built natively for Windows, it combines an advanced text editor with a secure standalone file system, ensuring unmatched speed and absolute privacy for your data.

What makes dbNotes unique:

The dbMoney Story

dbMoney Personal began over 15 years ago as a tool created to meet a personal need: organizing financial information with performance, flexibility, and privacy.

Over the years, the project has continuously evolved, gaining features, automation, dashboards, OFX import, and several customization options.


What started as a personal solution has transformed into the dbMoney Personal we know today.
